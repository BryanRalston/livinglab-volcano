Why is the flyer different?

The T. rex and the Triceratops are dinosaurs. They lived on land, so here they walk on the table. The one flying in a circle is a pterosaur — a flying reptile that lived at the same time. Pterosaurs are not dinosaurs. Dinosaurs did not have those long skin wings.
