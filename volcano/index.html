Craft Volcano

Place it on the table. Pour baking soda, then vinegar. Then say why.

Tap Place on table (AR) — stays in this page when WebXR is available. Place the craft volcano + cups, pour baking soda, then pour vinegar (foam). No 3D mesh. Use Record to save a clip of the plate (preview or AR).

Why did it foam?

Vinegar is an acid and baking soda is a base. When they mix they make carbon dioxide gas — tiny CO2 bubbles that swell into the foam that erupts over the crater rim.
