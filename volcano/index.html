Samsung screen recorder still captures AR passthrough if you need the camera itself.

Why did it foam?

Vinegar is an acid and baking soda is a base. When they mix they make carbon dioxide gas — tiny CO2 bubbles that swell into the foam that erupts over the crater rim.
